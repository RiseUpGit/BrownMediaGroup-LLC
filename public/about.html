# BrownMediaGroup LLC — Company Overview ## Mission Statement BrownMediaGroup exists at the intersection of hands-on technology and accessible media. Our mission is to build and share technology that works for real people—whether that means a family computer repaired and returned the same day, a YouTube channel that explains complex systems in plain language, or open, local-first software tools that put control back in the user’s hands. We are not a faceless corporation. We are a Michigan-based, owner-operated company built around three core pillars: professional PC repair and support, educational and entertainment technology content, and practical AI-powered tools designed for everyday workflows. Every decision we make—from how we structure our websites to how we design our software—revolves around transparency, ownership, and honest value. Our repair business delivers local pickup diagnostics, transparent pricing, and ongoing support contracts for residential and small business clients. Our YouTube channel documents the work: builds, unboxings, repairs, and deep dives into the kind of technology people actually own and operate. Our software division builds productized tools and agent frameworks that prioritize local storage, privacy, and user control over cloud dependency. BrownMediaGroup is committed to operating as a trusted, long-term presence in the technology space. We measure success not by funding rounds or acquisition targets, but by whether our customers feel confident in the systems we leave in their homes, whether our viewers learn something useful, and whether our tools help people work without surrendering their data or autonomy. --- ## About BrownMediaGroup LLC ### Who We Are BrownMediaGroup LLC is a Michigan-based technology company founded and operated by Joe Brown. We started as a one-person repair operation, grew into a content-driven media brand, and are now expanding into productized software and AI-assisted tools. The company is structured as a member-managed Michigan LLC, with all strategic decisions made directly by the founder. ### What We Do Our work spans three complementary areas: **PC Repair & Support** We provide residential and small business computer repair, diagnostics, and ongoing support. Our service model is built around transparency: we do on-site pickup, same-day diagnostics when possible, and clear pricing before any work starts. We also offer maintenance and support contracts for clients who want ongoing relationship rather than one-off Band-Aids. Our repair footprint is Southeast Michigan, but we serve clients online where appropriate for remote diagnostics and software support. **YouTube & Content Creation** Our YouTube channel is both a business funnel and an educational resource. Content ranges from repair tutorials and hardware unboxings to full system builds and behind-the-scenes looks at our homelab infrastructure. We document real configuration decisions, real failures, and real fixes—no sponsored vaporware. The channel is the primary public face of the company and the engine that drives new repair and tool customers. **AI & Developer Tools** Under the productized tools division, we design and ship software built for local-first operation. Our frameworks and agents are developed for real workflows—not demos. Current projects include standalone desktop inference harnesses, multi-model orchestration layers, and opinionated tooling for developers who want to own their stack. This work is informed by day-to-day use: the same team doing repairs and running homelabs builds the tools that support that work. ### Our Infrastructure We operate a physical homelab at our primary location, anchored by a Dell T3600 tower and a managed networking stack. That infrastructure hosts everything from Docker-based media and monitoring services to development builds and content-creation staging environments. The same cluster processes video renders, runs backups, and serves as a testbed for the AI tools we ship. ### Values - **Ownership:** We believe people should own their technology, their data, and their workflow. We don’t build products that lock users into subscriptions or cloud dependencies by accident. - **Transparency:** Our pricing, our infrastructure decisions, and our software choices are documented publicly when possible. Hidden behavior is a red flag we try not to raise ourselves. - **Realism:** We ship tested, working builds. We don’t release demos for attention, and we don’t pad feature sets with vapor. If it doesn’t work in production, we don’t ship it. - **Local presence:** We are based in Burton, Michigan, and we serve clients who can shake our hand. That geographic anchor matters to how we operate. ### Contact & Availability For repair inquiries, visit [ppcrepair.com](https://ppcrepair.com).
