Mission Statement

BrownMediaGroup is a family-owned and family-operated technology company based in Burton, Michigan. Joe Brown founded the company with a simple idea: bring honest, transparent tech services to people who don’t want to deal with big-box indifference. What started as a one-person repair operation has grown into a family team—Joe at the helm, son Alex handling repairs, teardown, and on-camera work, daughter Lily managing books, scheduling, and video production with CapCut and the full content pipeline. We are not a faceless corporation. We are a family building technology that works for real people.

Our mission is to build and share technology that serves actual needs—whether that means a family computer repaired and returned the same day, a YouTube channel that explains complex systems in plain language, or open, local-first software tools that put control back in the user’s hands. Every decision we make—from how we structure our websites to how we design our software—revolves around transparency, ownership, and honest value.

Our repair business delivers local pickup diagnostics, transparent pricing, and ongoing support contracts for residential and small business clients. Our YouTube channel documents the work: builds, unboxings, repairs, and deep dives into the kind of technology people actually own and operate. Our software division builds productized tools and agent frameworks that prioritize local storage, privacy, and user control over cloud dependency.

BrownMediaGroup is committed to operating as a trusted, long-term presence in the technology space. We measure success not by funding rounds or acquisition targets, but by whether our customers feel confident in the systems we leave in their homes, whether our viewers learn something useful, and whether our tools help people work without surrendering their data or autonomy.

What We Do

Our work spans three complementary areas:

PC Repair & Support

We provide residential and small business computer repair, diagnostics, and ongoing support. Our service model is built around transparency: we offer local pickup, same-day diagnostics when possible, and clear pricing before any work starts. We also offer maintenance and support contracts for clients who want an ongoing relationship rather than one-off fixes. Our repair footprint is Southeast Michigan, but we serve clients online where appropriate for remote diagnostics and software support.

YouTube & Content Creation

Our YouTube channel is both a business funnel and an educational resource. Content ranges from repair tutorials and hardware unboxings to full system builds and behind-the-scenes looks at our homelab infrastructure. We document real configuration decisions, real failures, and real fixes—no sponsored vaporware. The channel is the primary public face of the company and the engine that drives new repair and tool customers.

AI & Developer Tools

Under the productized tools division, we design and ship software built for local-first operation. Our frameworks and agents are developed for real workflows—not demos. Current projects include standalone desktop inference harnesses, multi-model orchestration layers, and opinionated tooling for developers who want to own their stack. This work is informed by day-to-day use: the same team doing repairs and running homelabs builds the tools that support that work.

Our Infrastructure

We operate a physical homelab anchored by a Dell T3600 tower and a managed networking stack. That infrastructure hosts Docker-based media and monitoring services, development builds, and content-creation staging environments. The same cluster processes video renders, runs backups, and serves as a testbed for the AI tools we ship.

Values

  • Ownership: We believe people should own their technology, their data, and their workflow. We don’t build products that lock users into subscriptions or cloud dependencies by accident.
  • Transparency: Our pricing, infrastructure decisions, and software choices are documented publicly when possible. Hidden behavior is a red flag we try not to raise ourselves.
  • Realism: We ship tested, working builds. We don’t release demos for attention, and we don’t pad feature sets with vapor. If it doesn’t work in production, we don’t ship it.
  • Local presence: We are based in Burton, Michigan, and we serve clients who can shake our hand. That geographic anchor matters to how we operate.
